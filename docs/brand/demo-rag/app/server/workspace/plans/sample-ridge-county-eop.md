# Sample Ridge County EOP

Sample Ridge County Emergency Operations Plan. Written for this demo; not a
real county's plan.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This plan covers emergencies in Sample Ridge County and the county's support
to its neighbours under mutual aid.

## 2. Concept of operations

The County Administrator activates the EOC on the Emergency Manager's advice.

## 3. Mutual aid

Sample Ridge County lends shelter space and heavy equipment to Sample Lakeview
County on request. The requesting county tracks the hours, equipment and costs
of every request and reimburses them within 90 days.

## 4. Plan maintenance

The Emergency Manager reviews this plan every two years and after every
exercise or activation, and records each change in the record of changes.
