# Sample Lakeview County EOP

Sample Lakeview County Emergency Operations Plan. Written for this demo; not a
real county's plan.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This plan sets out how Sample Lakeview County prepares for, responds to and
recovers from emergencies of any kind within the county's borders. It assumes
that county resources may be overwhelmed in a major flood or winter storm and
that state support may take 72 hours to arrive.

## 2. Hazards

The county's main hazards are riverine flooding along the Lakeview River,
severe winter storms, tornadoes and hazardous materials releases on Highway 9.

## 3. Concept of operations

The Emergency Management Director activates the emergency operations center
(EOC) when an incident exceeds the capacity of a single department. The EOC
runs at three levels: monitoring, partial and full activation. The county
moves from response to recovery when the Director declares life-safety
operations complete.

## 4. Assignment of responsibilities

The Sheriff's Office leads evacuation and traffic control. Public Works leads
debris clearance and road closures. The Health Department leads shelter health
and medical needs. Lakeview Schools provide buildings for shelters.

## 5. Communications

Responders use the county's radio system on the shared emergency talkgroup.
The public is warned by outdoor sirens and posts on the county website. The
plan does not say how warnings reach residents who are deaf or hard of hearing
or who read other languages, and it names no backup if the radio system fails.

## 6. Mutual aid

The county has a mutual aid agreement with Sample Ridge County for shelter
space and heavy equipment.

## 7. Plan maintenance

The Emergency Management Director keeps this plan and updates it as needed.

## 8. Authorities

State Emergency Management Act; Lakeview County Ordinance 14-2.
