Align on AG-UI 1.0.2 and CopilotKit 1.77.1. `@b4run/ag-ui` and `@b4run/cli` now depend on `@ag-ui/core` (and `@ag-ui/encoder`) 1.0.2, the release CopilotKit 1.77.1 and `@copilotkit/angular` 0.5.3 are built on, so an app using them resolves one `@ag-ui/core` version with B4.run. The navlog scaffold generated by `create-b4-app` pins `@ag-ui/client` 1.0.2 and `@copilotkit/react-core`/`@copilotkit/runtime` 1.77.1. The optional peer ranges are unchanged: `@ag-ui/client` `>=1.0.1 <2.0.0` and `@copilotkit/react-core` `>=1.76.0`.
