The navlog template's route bar reorders waypoints: drag a pill along the bar (hold it first on a touch screen), or focus its identifier and press the arrow keys, Home or End. The map previews the new order while you drag, an insertion line marks where the pill lands, Escape cancels, and a live region announces each move.
