# CPG 101 planning process

Excerpt adapted from FEMA CPG 101 v3.1, Developing and Maintaining Emergency
Operations Plans. A short paraphrase of a US government work, for this demo.

## Step 1: Form a collaborative planning team

Planning works best as a team effort that brings in the whole community:
emergency management, public safety, public health, schools, utilities,
nonprofit and private partners, and people who represent residents with
disabilities and others with access and functional needs.

## Step 2: Understand the situation

The team identifies the threats and hazards the jurisdiction faces, assesses
the risk they pose, and names the community's resources and vulnerabilities.

## Step 3: Determine goals and objectives

The team sets the operational priorities and the goals and objectives the plan
must achieve for each hazard and phase of an incident.

## Step 4: Plan development

The team develops courses of action, identifies the resources each one needs,
and confirms that the plan is feasible, acceptable, adequate and complete.

## Step 5: Plan preparation, review and approval

The team writes the plan, reviews it for compliance with applicable laws and
guidance, has it approved by the jurisdiction's leadership, and distributes it.

## Step 6: Plan implementation and maintenance

The plan is trained and exercised, and it is reviewed and revised on a regular
cycle, such as every two years, and after every exercise or real incident.
