# CPG 101 basic plan elements

Excerpt adapted from FEMA CPG 101 v3.1, Developing and Maintaining Emergency
Operations Plans. A short paraphrase of a US government work, for this demo.

## Basic plan elements

A basic plan gives an overview of the jurisdiction's approach to emergency
operations. It typically contains the elements below.

## Purpose, scope, situation and assumptions

States why the plan exists, what and whom it covers, the hazards and
conditions it addresses, and what the planners assumed to be true.

## Concept of operations

Explains how the jurisdiction responds as a whole: who decides, when the
emergency operations center activates, and how operations move from response
to recovery.

## Organization and assignment of responsibilities

Assigns each task to an organization or position, so every agency knows what
it is expected to do.

## Communications

Describes how responders communicate with each other and how the jurisdiction
warns and informs the public, including people with disabilities and those
who need information in other languages, with backups if primary systems fail.

## Administration, finance and logistics

Covers how records are kept, how costs are tracked and reimbursed, how mutual
aid is requested, and how resources are ordered and managed during an
incident.

## Plan development and maintenance

Names who owns the plan and sets a regular cycle to review and update it, such
as every two years and after exercises and real incidents.

## Authorities and references

Lists the laws, regulations and agreements the plan rests on.
